# Assignment 01 — Boundary-Layer Similarity Solutions & Orr–Sommerfeld Stability

Two cases, both run from one entry point (`main.py`). The Orr–Sommerfeld case always computes its Blasius base flow first.

| Case | Equation | What is computed |
|---|---|---|
| **Falkner–Skan** (stagnation point) | $f''' + ff'' - (f')^2 + 1 = 0$ | $f''(0)$, profiles, $\delta^*$, $\theta$, $H$ with three methods |
| **Orr–Sommerfeld** (Blasius, parallel flow) | stage 1: $f''' + \tfrac12 ff'' = 0$ (three methods)<br>stage 2: $(U-c)(\phi''-\alpha^2\phi) - U''\phi = \frac{1}{i\alpha Re}(\phi''''-2\alpha^2\phi''+\alpha^4\phi)$ | Blasius base flow, then eigenvalue $c(\alpha, Re)$ by shooting from $y_\infty$ to the wall, growth-rate map, neutral curve, critical $Re$ |

All with $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=0$, $f'(\infty)=1$ for the similarity problems, and $\phi(0)=\phi'(0)=0$, $\phi\to0$ as $y\to\infty$ for Orr–Sommerfeld.

## Orr–Sommerfeld method in short

1. **Base flow** — Blasius equation solved three ways (stage 1); Blasius $f''(0)$ from `solve_bvp`, re-integrated with DOP853 and rescaled so lengths are in $\delta^*$ ($Re = Re_{\delta^*}$).
2. **Start at $y_\infty$** (= 8 $\delta^*$): in the freestream $U = 1$, so the two decaying solutions are $e^{-\alpha y}$ and $e^{-\gamma y}$, $\gamma^2 = \alpha^2 + i\alpha Re(1-c)$.
3. **Integrate down to the wall** with RK4, carrying the 2×2 minors of the two solutions (compound-matrix method), which avoids parasitic growth of the viscous solution.
4. **Wall condition** $\phi(0) = \phi'(0) = 0$ ⇔ $D(\alpha, Re, c) = \phi_1\phi_2' - \phi_2\phi_1' = 0$ at $y = 0$, solved for complex $c$ by Newton–Raphson.
5. **Sweep** a log-$Re$ × $\alpha$ grid with continuation of the TS mode, contour $c_i = 0$, refine each point (real $c$, 2×2 Newton), and minimise $Re$ on the curve for the critical point.
